## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Ability to connect to all the tools I asked Juno so I can get customer feedback crowdsourced from different sources.
- **Value proposition:** Turn all the info from the resources into meaningful buckets. Provide meaninful roadmap.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data freshness | Is the data or tkts produced /crowdsourced fresh or stale. Anything past 6 months is considered old.  | Yes / No | It means data is fresh. | n/a | Anything past 6 months is considered stale |
| Hallucinate | Are you making up your tkt numbers or they are truly coming from the resources.  | Yes / No | _ | n/a | _ |
| Permissions | Does Juno have the right permissions to access the requested apps / systems | Yes / No | _ | n/a | _ |
| Sentiment / tone (deal-breaker) | Is the tone of the content provided in the app making sense or needs to be reviewed. | 1 to 5 | _ | _ | _ |
| Performance | Is the app responding slow. is the latency as expected. | 1 to 5 | _ | _ | _ |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 answers per question per week
- **Who scores them with you:** Me and another PM, possibly another agent
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Take the sample of the disagreed responses, pass it to another agent

## Pass bar

Number of Yes or Nos depending on the type of questions. Some responses are No

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Sentiment / tone: any score of 1
